# UPL Top Scorers Analysis — Season 2025/26

## Project Overview

This Power BI report analyzes the leading goalscorers in the Ukrainian Premier League during the 2025/26 season. The project compares players using both total output and efficiency-based indicators to provide a broader view of goalscoring performance.

## Project Objective

The objective is to evaluate and rank attacking performance using several complementary metrics rather than relying only on total goals.

## Key Metrics

- **Goals** — total goals scored by the player.
- **Goals per 90** — goals scored per 90 minutes played.
- **Non-Penalty Goals** — goals scored excluding penalties.
- **Goal Contribution** — the player's goals as a share of the team's total goals.

## Ranking Methodology

Players receive an individual rank for each metric. The final composite score is calculated as:

**Overall Score = Goals Rank + Goals per 90 Rank + Non-Penalty Goals Rank + Goal Contribution Rank**

The player with the lowest overall score receives the highest position in the composite ranking.

To ensure a more reliable comparison, only players with at least **600 minutes played** are included in the Goals per 90 and Overall Composite rankings.

## Report Highlights

- Top 10 overall scorers based on the composite ranking.
- Top 5 players by total goals.
- Top 5 players by goals per 90 minutes.
- Top 5 players by non-penalty goals.
- Top 5 players by goal contribution.
- **Andrii Storchous (FC Kudrivka)** leads the overall composite ranking in this report.
