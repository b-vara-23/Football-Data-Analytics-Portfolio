# Football Scouting and Analysis Coursework

Selected coursework completed during my football scouting, performance analysis and data analysis studies. These reports show how I assess players and teams, translate observations into tactical recommendations, and design training activities.

These are educational exercises and independent analyses. References to FC Barcelona, Real Madrid or their departments describe the subject or hypothetical scenario of a report; they do not imply employment by or endorsement from either club. The sample opponent in the PFSA report is fictional.
